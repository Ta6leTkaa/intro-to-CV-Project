# Construction-PPE

Source: [Ultralytics Construction-PPE](https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/detect/construction-ppe/).
Archive details and checksums: [source.json](source.json).
The official class mapping is saved in [upstream-construction-ppe.yaml](upstream-construction-ppe.yaml).
The publisher specifies AGPL-3.0; the license file from the archive is preserved as
[LICENSE-Construction-PPE.txt](LICENSE-Construction-PPE.txt).

Local preparation preserves the images and original class IDs. Ten label files without
images and one duplicate box were excluded from the prepared copy. A total of 149 images
were reassigned between splits according to the saved scene review decisions.
The source archive and its extracted copy are unchanged.
